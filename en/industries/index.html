Industries

Legal and financial advice tailored to the regulation and risks of each industry.

Technology, media and telecommunications (TMT)

We know the regulations that apply to this industry and have trained in antitrust, consumer protection, advertising, intellectual property, personal data and the regulatory framework of the telecommunications and information technology authorities, as well as in the implementation of innovative and disruptive business models.

Retail

We provide specialized services for the challenges of the retail industry, including advice on commercial contracts, regulatory compliance, risk management and dispute resolution.

Fintech

We advise from the early stages of product and service development and design, across the technological, financial and regulatory fields, drawing on our knowledge of the debate and passage of Mexico’s Fintech Law and its practical application.

Nearshoring

Mexico holds a strategic position for relocating operations to the North American region. We advise and assist our clients on the legal side of setting up and operating in the country.

E-commerce

We know the regulatory framework of digital business: apps, social media and e-commerce platforms, among others. Our training in intellectual property, industrial property, telecommunications and information technology allows us to serve this industry comprehensively.

Healthcare

We trained as advisors to the pharmaceutical industry, from production to distribution and sales. We support our clients, legally and financially, in decision-making and in applying the regulatory framework, with a focus on risk management and the prevention of legal and financial contingencies. We also know the private hospital sector and its commercial relationships with insurers, medical laboratories and patients, under health and commercial regulations.

Education

We provide strategic, tailored legal solutions to private education businesses, focused on municipal, state and federal regulatory compliance, with a preventive legal and financial approach to mitigate risk.

Energy

We advise and assist energy projects nationwide: development and operation of power, wind, solar and clean energy projects, hydrocarbons and service stations, as well as contract management and compliance with their regulatory obligations.

Hospitality and tourism

We advise from the initial legal and financial planning stage on the acquisition, establishment, development, construction and operation of tourism projects: hotels, vacation clubs, private residential clubs, timeshare and mixed-use projects.

Entertainment and sports

We provide comprehensive advice on the organization, production and execution of sports, cultural and entertainment events: design, drafting, review and negotiation of sponsorship agreements, sports venue leases, advertising, brand activations, commercial partnerships and the hiring of artistic and sports talent and public figures.

Real estate

We carry out legal and financial analysis for property acquisitions and risk management in leases and subleases, as well as land-use regulation and development under urban plans and the applicable legal framework. We also analyze, from a legal and financial standpoint, debt or equity financing options for your acquisition or lease project.
